# Demo script — Plain Privacy (under 3 minutes)

Total runtime: 2 minutes 55 seconds. Record the spoken lines below over the
actions named in each scene.

## Scene 1 — What it is (0:00–0:15)

Show the app home screen.

> This is Plain Privacy. You paste a privacy policy, a permission list, or a
> data export — and you get a short answer in plain words: what is collected,
> who gets it, and what you can turn off. Nothing leaves the phone.

## Scene 2 — A sample policy, answered at once (0:15–0:55)

Paste a sample policy. Tap Explain. The three sections appear.

> Here is a sample policy. I paste it, tap Explain, and the three sections
> appear at once. What is collected: location and contacts. Who gets it: third
> parties and advertisers. What you can turn off: location in settings, and
> deleting your data. No model, no network — this is the on-phone check, and
> it runs anywhere.

## Scene 3 — A permission list (0:55–1:35)

Paste a short permission list. Tap Explain again.

> A second sample: an app permission list. Same three sections. The list asks
> for the camera and the microphone — the answer names them, names who
> receives them, and names the permission you can switch off. If the pasted
> text does not name something, the answer says so plainly instead of guessing.

## Scene 4 — The on-phone model, optional (1:35–2:10)

Tap "Load on-phone model". Show the loading status, then Explain again.

> The on-phone model is optional. One tap, on Wi-Fi, downloads it once —
> about 600 to 800 megabytes. After that it refines the wording. If it cannot
> load on a phone, the check still answers — the app never leaves you with a
> blank screen.

## Scene 5 — Airplane mode proof (2:10–2:45)

Turn on Airplane mode. Close the app fully. Reopen it from the home screen.
Paste again. The answer appears.

> Now Airplane mode. I close the app, reopen it, paste again — and the answer
> appears with no network at all. The model, once kept on the phone, works the
> same way. Nothing you pasted went anywhere: it stayed on the phone.

## Scene 6 — Close (2:45–2:55)

Show the three sections on screen, then the credit line.

> Plain Privacy. Your privacy data, in plain words. Wyndham Heaven (elghaly).
