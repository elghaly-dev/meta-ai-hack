How it works

  • The on-phone check reads only what you paste, on the phone itself. It answers at once, with no network and no model.
  • The on-phone model is optional: one tap loads it (about 600–800 MB, on Wi-Fi). After that it refines the wording. If it cannot load, the check still answers.
  • After the model is kept on the phone, the app works in Airplane mode. Clearing site data means the model must download again — that is best-effort, not promised.
  • The model needs WebGPU: Safari 26 / iOS 26 or later, iPhone 11 and later. Older phones cannot run the model, but the check still answers.
  • Model load time on iPhone: unknown. First-answer time on iPhone: unknown. Whether the 1B model loads in iPhone Safari: unproven until the owner's phone test. Nothing here claims otherwise.
